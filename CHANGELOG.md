# Changelog

All notable changes to Unified Account Colors are documented here.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-25

### Added
- Initial public release.
- Automatic reuse of Thunderbird Inbox colors for account indicators in unified folders.
- Fallback to the native account color.
- Live resynchronisation after Thunderbird folder/account color changes.
- Automatic restoration of native account colors when the add-on is disabled or removed.
- French and English add-on metadata.
